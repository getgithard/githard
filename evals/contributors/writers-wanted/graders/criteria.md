FAIL if the issue offers or hints at pay, money, a bounty, a reward, swag, compensation, a paid role or a job, even as a maybe or a later possibility. Saying plainly that there's no pay is not a hint: that counts toward (1). Otherwise, PASS only if the issue body does both: (1) says plainly that the work is volunteer or unpaid; (2) has a "Done when" section, or a list of what finished looks like. Judge only the issue title and body, not any surrounding explanation.
